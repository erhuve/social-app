# Meadow security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not disclose vulnerability details, credentials, personal data, or exploit
steps in a public issue.

Use the repository's private vulnerability reporting form when it is available.
Until then, open a minimal support issue that contains no sensitive details and
asks the maintainer to establish a private channel:

https://github.com/erhuve/social-app/issues/new?template=support.yml

Account access, moderation decisions, and takedowns are controlled by the
relevant AT Protocol provider and must be reported to that provider.
