# Meadow Support

Meadow is an independent, experimental AT Protocol client and is not operated
by or affiliated with Bluesky Social PBC.

For Meadow client or multiplicity problems, open a private-safe issue in the
[Meadow repository](https://github.com/erhuve/social-app/issues/new). Do not
include passwords, app passwords, access tokens, private messages, email
addresses, or other sensitive information.

Account access, moderation decisions, takedowns, and content reports are handled
by the AT Protocol service or moderation provider selected by the user. Use the
in-app reporting and appeal flows for those issues.
