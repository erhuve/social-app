# Meadow Community Guidelines

Meadow does not host AT Protocol accounts or posts. Content and conduct are
governed and moderated by the hosting, AppView, labeler, and moderation services
selected by the user.

Use Meadow's in-app report and appeal flows to contact the relevant moderation
provider. Do not use Meadow to harass people, compromise accounts, evade service
rules, or interfere with the network or the beta.

For a problem in the Meadow client itself, see [Meadow Support](./support.md).
